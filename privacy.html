Privacy policy

Dondo · Last updated 7 October 2026

Dondo is a private AI assistant used by a single person, its owner. This policy explains how Dondo accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

Data Dondo accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's consent screen, Dondo accesses the owner's Google Calendar with read and write access: calendars, event details (titles, times, locations, descriptions, attendees) and free/busy information.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to carry out the owner's requests, such as answering questions about their schedule or creating, updating and deleting events. Dondo does not use it for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to those requests.

How the data is processed and stored

  • Dondo runs on a private server controlled by the owner.
  • To answer a request, relevant calendar details may be sent to the third-party AI model provider that powers the assistant, solely to generate that response.
  • Calendar details may remain in the assistant's conversation history and notes on the owner's private server.
  • OAuth tokens are stored on that server and are used only to call Google Calendar on the owner's behalf.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, except the AI model processing described above, and only as needed to fulfil the owner's own requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Dondo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Dondo does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. After revocation, Dondo can no longer read or change the calendar, and the owner can delete stored tokens and conversation history from their server.
