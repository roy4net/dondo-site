Dondo

A private, single-user AI assistant.

Dondo is a personal assistant run by its owner for their own use. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public. No sign-up is available.

What it does with Google Calendar

When the owner connects their Google account, Dondo can read and manage their own calendar on request, for example:

  • looking up upcoming events and free time,
  • creating events and reminders the owner asks for,
  • updating or cancelling events the owner asks it to change.

Dondo acts only on the account that authorized it, and only on the owner's request.
